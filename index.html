Privacy Policy – Linux Master

Effective date: 17 November 2026

Linux Master ("the app") is an educational app for learning Linux commands. This policy explains how the app handles information. In short: the app does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data.

Information the app stores

To track your learning progress, the app saves the following only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • The display name you optionally type during setup
  • Lessons and challenges you have completed, XP, streaks, quiz statistics and achievements
  • Your settings (theme, terminal text size, daily goal)

Commands you type in the practice terminal run inside a simulation in the app's memory. They are not sent anywhere and do not access your phone's real files.

Information the app does not collect

The app has no account system, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party SDKs. It does not request the internet permission or any other Android permission, so it cannot transmit data. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Backups

If you have enabled Android's backup feature, Android may include the app's progress data in your personal Google device backup so it can be restored on a new phone. This is controlled by your device settings and Google's privacy policy; the developer has no access to it.

Deleting your data

You can erase all progress at any time from Profile → Settings → Reset all progress, or by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
